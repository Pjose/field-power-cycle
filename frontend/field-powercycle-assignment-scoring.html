Ranked candidates
How this score is built. Each component is normalized 0–1 before weighting, so a technician 2 minutes away doesn't automatically dominate one who's the only certified match. Proximity comes from live GPS + current route ETA, not straight-line distance. Certification match is binary-gated first — an uncertified technician is excluded from the list entirely, not just scored lower, since a payment-terminal job shouldn't go to someone without that credential regardless of how close they are. Current load reflects how full the rest of their day already is. SLA headroom checks whether taking this job would put their existing assignments at risk of breaching, not just whether this new ticket makes it in time.
